🔧 Troubleshooting Steps

  • 1 Check that the drive is physically connected to the USB port
  • 2 Verify the drive LED is on (indicates power)
  • 3 Wait 10-30 seconds for the system to auto-mount the drive
  • 4 If not auto-mounted, try unplugging and reconnecting the drive
  • 5 Check system logs with dmesg | tail if drive isn't detected
